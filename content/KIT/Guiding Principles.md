1. [[Linking Your Thinking]]
   - Your brain uses relations and links to learn, remember and understand and so should your vault.
2. Your Vault is your forest and it needs maintenance, but don't overdo it.
   - Make sure to weed your forest, but don't do it unless you encounter a note/idea. Going through your notes just for the sake of maintaining them is not the best idea.
3. Order from chaos
   - Don't think too much about where you are putting notes, rather focus on capturing data.
4. Dynamicity of data
   - Data should be dynamically retrievable. Utilizing the [[YAML Front Matter|YAML Frontmatter]] as well as other aspects of a note.
