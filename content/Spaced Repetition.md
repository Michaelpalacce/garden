Spaced repetition is the **only effective form** of [[Repetition|repetition]], usually **with the help of flashcards**, and can be used to [[Continuous Retrieval|strengthen]] the **retrieval of information**.

The general difference is the process of which repetition is done. In spaced repetition, **questions are prepared by you for you**, with the aim to **extract core knowledge** that will **help revitalize** your [[Linking Your Thinking|connections]] that may or may not have been lost.

_From personal experience this is a semi effective method of learning for me._
